SentinelTrace

Python-based SSH authentication log monitoring & threat detection

Designed to reduce manual review of authentication logs by detecting repeated SSH failures, a successful login after a failure burst, and remote root access patterns. The tool prioritizes alerts and generates JSON, CSV and HTML outputs for first-level analyst review.
